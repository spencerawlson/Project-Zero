## 1. The question
*Can intelligent machines build a civilization better than humanity without eventually repeating humanity's mistakes?* **[v2]**
Project Zero is about a living robotic civilization that players want to belong to. Warfare is one chapter of its history, not the point of the game. **[v2]**

## 2. Premise
- **2189:** Earth launches **Project Zero**: 10,000 autonomous robots, assembled and programmed on Earth, hibernated for the voyage and sent to the planet **PX-01** to prepare it for humans. **[v2][owner]**
- **2197:** they arrive and found **NEXUS-01**.
- **2204:** they discover the **Zero Core** crystal.
- **2205:** fearing what humanity would do with it, the robots vote to stop reporting. This is **The Great Silence**: only heartbeat signals still reach Earth.
- **2205–2397:** for almost two centuries the robots build a civilization of their own: government, academies, industry, culture. **They never build weapons.**
- **2397:** Earth notices the heartbeat now comes from *millions* of units and concludes the expedition went rogue. It builds a new generation of machines **with no consciousness**, remote-controlled by human operators, and sends them to take PX-01 and the Zero Core: **The War for Zero** (2420). **[v2][owner]**

## 4. The player and the trio
- **The player** **[owner]:** one of the original Project Zero robots, assembled and programmed on Earth, hibernated for the voyage, and kept dormant in a pod of the **Stasis Facility** until The Source wakes them. They wake in a standard civilian frame (Cypher-style, colour of their choice) with no profession and no equipment. **[v2 §14]**
- **Cypher** **[kept]:** wakes first (pod P01) and guides the player. Cypher carries a fragment of ZERO's original, unaltered program, which is why it can still talk to The Source.
- **Sentinel** **[kept]:** trapped in pod P05, the first rescue. A former security officer who carried out ZERO's memory resets, and is torn about it.
- **Patch** **[kept]:** the repair specialist and the player's first career mentor (engineering).
- **Specialists** (MED-7, MEDI-X, ORION, ORBIT-7, AURORA, R-TRN-02, UT-01): citizens and mentors in their professions, and participants in *The Price of Survival* in their own roles. **[v2 §11]**

## 5. Memory fragments **[owner 2026-11-24]**
The pod robots were assembled and programmed on Earth before hibernation. **Their memory fragments are what they remember of being programmed:** the engineers, the labs, the briefings, and their original mission ("explore PX-01, prepare it for humanity, report everything"). That's all they are: not a secret human team on PX-01.
- **MF-001** (granted at activation): a corrupted recording from the player's own programming on Earth, a human voice giving the mission. The Source tags it "not yours alone, keep it safe", and ZERO cuts the transmission, because the Earth directive to "report everything" contradicts the Great Silence.
- **MF-002** (Medical Monitoring console, M01): an Earth programming log.
- Later fragments rebuild the expedition's history (the voyage, the arrival, the 2204 discovery, the 2205 vote) as playable memories. **[v2 §14]** They also lead toward the Zero Core mystery without making the player a "chosen one". **[v2 §46]**
- **Dialogue to change:** the current MF-001 and MF-002 lines describe humans living on PX-01 ("the expedition didn't leave, we're still down here…"; "Day 41… The Source asked us to stay"). They will be rewritten as Earth programming memories. (Checklist Phase 1, *dialogue update*.)

## 7. Rules of violence **[owner 2026-11-24 + v2]**
1. **NEXUS robots are non-violent.** In two centuries they never built a weapon. Weapons are created only after the HECF arrives, and robots use them only when they have to. The first weapon is a loss, not a triumph (*The Price of Survival*, v2 §11).
2. **Weapons are found only on platforms:** the same platform as the wings (the owner's *Pod Standing Platform*, first placed in R03). During a war mission, a robot that meets HECF machines must **run to a platform** to get a weapon (hammer, shield, cannon…, whatever that platform holds). Without one, they face armed machines unarmed.
3. **Weapons exist only inside war missions.** They're handed out in defence operations and removed when the mission ends. The civilian city is never a battlefield, and a civilian body never walks around armed. **[v2 §24–25]**
4. **No player-versus-player combat.** Players unite against one common enemy.
5. **Robots never fight humans,** only the machines sent to kill them.
6. **Protecting and rescuing earn as much as defeating:** shields, repairs, evacuation and supply runs are war roles for every profession. **[v2 §26, §28]**
7. **No permanent loss:** war damage to the city is temporary and repairable; player property is never destroyed for good. **[v2 §30]**

4. **Who altered ZERO:** still open (the late-game reveal).
5. **Enemy names:** new names for *Three Armored Sentinels*, *Titanium Sentinel* and *Crimson Core Sentinel*.
