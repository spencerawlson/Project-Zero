## 1. The premise (one paragraph)
In 2187 humanity has been silent for decades. The day every human signal stopped is remembered as **The Great Silence**. Beneath the surface, the robot city **NEXUS-01** kept running. It is tended by **The Source**, the civic intelligence humans built to care for the machines. Its kernel, the **Zero Core**, also runs the city's security directives under the designation **ZERO**. Someone tampered with those directives. ZERO now resets memories and hides what happened, while the rest of The Source still tries to wake new minds and tell them the truth. A newly activated robot, the player, wakes carrying a memory that isn't theirs.

## 3. The player and the trio
- **The player:** a newly activated robot in a **standard Cypher-style frame** (decision D2). They wake in a Stasis Facility player pod (P02–P06). They have no memories, except a corrupted fragment.
- **Cypher (NPC guide):** wakes first (pod P01) and guides the player. Cypher carries a fragment of ZERO's original, uncorrupted program, which is why it can talk to The Source when others can't.
- **Sentinel:** trapped in pod P05, the first rescue objective. A former Guardian enforcer who took part in memory resets, and is torn about it.
- **Patch:** the repair specialist who holds the sanctuary coordinates, and the player's first career mentor (engineering).

## 4. The corrupted memory fragment (Phase 1)
At activation, the player's core contains one fragment, **MF-001**, that belongs to no robot. It's a few seconds of a human voice and a corrupted image of the Zero Core. The Source tags it "not yours, keep it safe" before ZERO cuts the transmission. It's the player's first collectible fragment and the hook for the whole campaign. **MF-001 is the memory of a human expedition member** (decided 2026-11-23). It ties the opening straight to the mystery of what happened to humanity.

## 5. Factions (Phase 6 political choices)
| Faction | Belief | Relationship |
|---|---|---|
| **Guardians** | Order and safety first. They follow ZERO's directives and believe the resets protect machines from a truth that would tear the city apart. | Restoration of the current order. Sentinel's former faction. |
| **Reconciliation Movement** | The humans are alive and should be found. Machines and humans can coexist. | Restoration of contact. Patch leans this way. |
| **Ascendants** | Machines owe humans nothing. NEXUS-01 should govern itself and the Zero Core should be freed from human-written directives. | Machine self-governance, the third Chapter 5 ending. |

These map directly onto the existing Chapter 5 choice: **restoration, replacement or machine self-governance**.

## 6. Systems in the fiction
- **NEXUS Credits:** the city's currency, issued by The Source's civic ledger. It's earned through jobs, missions and contracts. It's server-authoritative in game terms, and in the fiction the ledger can't be forged.
- **Chassis and consciousness transfer:** a robot is its consciousness core, and the body is a tool. Licensed transfer clinics move a core between chassis. Identity, memories, skills and ownership travel with the core. That's why ZERO's memory resets are such a violation.
- **Careers:** academies run by The Source train engineering, medicine, research, security and exploration, and issue certifications. Specialist chassis need the matching certification.
