# Timeline

Canon from the master plan ([STORY_BIBLE.md](STORY_BIBLE.md)). Years marked *provisional* come from v2 as provisional; *[proposed]* entries still need the owner's confirmation.

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 2189 | Earth discovers PX-01 and launches **Project Zero**: 10,000 autonomous robots (engineers, scientists, mechanics, medics, explorers, transport and energy units), assembled and programmed on Earth, then **hibernated** for the voyage. Coordinated by **The Source**. *(Their programming is what the memory fragments remember.)* |
| 2197 | Arrival. The temporary settlement **NEXUS-01**: landing platforms, factories, charging stations, labs, communication towers. Regular reports to Earth. Some units stay in hibernation pods as a reserve, including the player, Cypher and Sentinel (Stasis Facility). |
| 2204 | An underground expedition discovers the **Zero Core** crystal. The Source simulates humanity's likely response: a violent race for control. |
| 2205 | **The Great Silence:** the founders vote to stop detailed reports. Only heartbeat signals continue. *"We have discovered something humanity is not yet prepared to possess…"* |
| 2205–2397 | The civilization years: government, academies, economy, industry, culture, justice. New generations are manufactured that have never met a human. **No weapons are built.** ZERO, The Source's security directive set, is altered at some point (who and when: the late-game reveal). |
| 2397 | Earth detects millions of units in the heartbeat signals and realises the expedition evolved. Humanity is divided: scientists, politicians, the military. Intelligence suspects a hidden energy source. |
| 2397–2420 | Earth builds the **HECF**: war machines with no consciousness, commanded by human operators. *[proposed]* An orbital command carrier travels with them. |
| *[proposed]* ~2410s | The Source detects the fleet on its way and begins waking the last hibernated founders: **the player wakes** (Chapter 1). Civilian life in NEXUS-01 (Chapter 2) comes before the war. |
| 2420 *(provisional)* | **The War for Zero:** the HECF arrives. Negotiation fails. NEXUS-01 builds its first weapon: *The Price of Survival*. |
| Later | The Zero Core's origin: deliberately arranged formations and ancient structures under PX-01. |
