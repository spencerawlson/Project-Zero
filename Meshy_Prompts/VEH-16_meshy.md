# VEH-16 Battle Walker: Meshy prompt

**Prompt:** white and orange bipedal battle walker mech, futuristic industrial sci-fi, graphite brushed metal panels, orange industrial accents, cyan emissive light strips, clean hard-surface panel lines, game-ready, no text, no logos

**Negative:** organic, rust holes, cartoon toy, glossy chrome, humans, text, watermark, base plate

**Reference images:** the orthographic renders on the VEH-16 sheet (front, side, top) plus any user concept named on the sheet.

**Target after Blender:** Body ≤ 8 k tris; wheels and rotors separate meshes with pivots at axles; 1024² trim + decals.

_Dimensions (14 tall (user model) studs) are design assumptions._
