## The ten decisions

   Founders' Quarter slice is retained. Supersedes the v1.3 350×350 hub plan
   requirement.
2. **Workshop ramp (§18a) — DEFERRED until a multistory workshop is needed.**
   The defect stays open until then; it is NOT closed as moot by this
   decision.
3. **Factory mission (§18b) — DEFERRED until the appropriate story or career
   phase.** No mission design is approved yet; the gap stays open as deferred.
4. **Elevators — INTRODUCE only when functional multistory buildings require
   them.** No elevator work before then.
5. **Energy Spire — RESERVED, inaccessible for now.** Kept for future
   exploration or story missions; no enterable Spire at launch.
6. **Scrap — RETIRED as a currency.** NEXUS Credits is the only currency.
   Scrap may be reconsidered later as a crafting resource, not as money.
7. **Flight — PRESERVED as approved civilian mobility.** Nightwing flight
   stays; no flight removal.
8. **Combat — PRESERVED as approved defensive warfare only.** Platform-based
   weapons, war missions only, no PvP, no fighting humans. Cypher's
   established role is preserved; any unresolved combat-role or narrative
   rule needs explicit clarification, no new canon invented.
9. **Planning documents — PRODUCED PROGRESSIVELY as each phase requires
   them.** The D10 status conflict (proposed vs settled) is to be reconciled
   in the governing documents.
10. **Asset Bible Rev D — APPROVED only after owner review.** No blanket
    approval is implied by this decision; Rev D's standards need a separate
    owner review before they carry authority.
